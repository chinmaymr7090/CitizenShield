1. Introduction

In simple words, CitizenShield is an intelligent municipal website that allows everyday citizens to report urban problems—such as broken roads, potholes, overflowing garbage dumps, pipe leaks, and drainage water—in less than one minute.

Unlike traditional government portals that ask citizens to fill out confusing multi-page forms, CitizenShield allows a person to simply snap a photograph on their phone. The computer system uses Real Artificial Intelligence (Computer Vision) to inspect the photograph, detect the exact defect, tag the GPS location, and send the complaint directly to the correct municipal engineer.

2. Problem Statement

Everyday citizens face major frustrations when reporting civic defects to city councils:

  • Wrong Department Routing: Citizens often don't know whether a street issue belongs to the Roads Division, the Water Board, or the Electricity Department (BESCOM). Complaints bounce around for weeks without action.
  • Fake Closures on Paper: Contractors and field workers often mark complaints as "Resolved" without actually doing the work, leaving citizens frustrated.
  • No Live Updates: Citizens have no way to know if anyone has visited the site, what the officer's remarks are, or when repair work will begin.
  • Duplicate Dispatches: Multiple neighbors often report the same pothole on the same road, causing municipal vans to waste time and fuel inspecting the same spot twice.

3. Literature Survey & 4. Project Gap

Modern academic research proves that deep neural networks (specifically YOLOv8 architectures) can identify surface distress like tarmac cracks and potholes with over 80% accuracy in real-world lighting conditions. Furthermore, spatial clustering algorithms (such as Haversine distance calculations) can detect whether two GPS coordinates are within 75 meters of each other.

The Research Gap Identified: Conventional state e-governance portals (such as generic municipal portals) merely store plain text descriptions. They lack real AI image verification at the time of upload, they do not provide a live visual danger map for city leaders, and critically, they do not enforce a mandatory post-repair photograph before closing a case.

5. Project Objectives & 6. Scope

Primary Objectives:

  1. Implement real deep learning image detection for 5 civic categories: Potholes, Road damage, Garbage accumulation, Drainage overflow, and Water leakage.
  2. Eliminate fake complaint closures by strictly requiring officers to upload an after-repair verification photo before marking any complaint as "Resolved".
  3. Provide citizens with a clear 10-Stage Vertical Milestone Tracker and direct official replies from municipal engineers.
  4. Generate a live colour-coded GIS map of Tumakuru with Green (Low), Orange (Medium), and Red (High Danger) monitoring zones.

Project Scope: The system covers all 35 Administrative Wards of Tumakuru City Corporation across 4 key departments: Roads & Civil Infrastructure, BESCOM Public Lighting, KUWSDB Water Supply & Sewerage, and Solid Waste Management.

7. Methodology & 8. System Design

CitizenShield operates on a clean 5-step operational pipeline:

  1. Citizen Lodgment: Citizen captures a photo; browser acquires GPS coordinates via HTML5 Geolocation API.
  2. AI Vision Analysis: YOLOv8 neural network and OpenCV spectral filters scan the image and determine category and severity.
  3. Proximity & Duplicate Check: System checks if another active grievance exists within 75 meters to prevent duplicate crew dispatches.
  4. Officer Action & Reply: Assigned officer views evidence, AI confidence score, and dispatches a repair crew while posting an official reply.
  5. Verified Resolution: Officer attaches a photo of the completed repair. The citizen's tracking page immediately updates to "Resolved".

9. Implementation Details

Key Architectural Features:
  • Multi-threaded Production Server: Powered by Python Flask and Waitress WSGI server for zero downtime.
  • Real AI Models: Custom-weighted YOLOv8 networks (pothole_yolov8n.pt, road_distress_yolov8n.pt, garbage_yolov8n.pt) + OpenCV Hydrological Analyzer.
  • Official Officer Directives: Direct officer-to-citizen messaging with instant in-app notification alerts.
  • 10-Stage Progress Pipeline: From 1. Submitted $\rightarrow$ 4. Officer Assigned $\rightarrow$ 8. Repair Started $\rightarrow$ 9. Resolved.
  • Strict Privacy (RBAC): Unauthorized citizens receive HTTP 403 Forbidden if trying to inspect another person's private grievance.

11. Conclusion & Future Scope

CitizenShield demonstrates that modern web systems and artificial intelligence can eliminate bureaucratic delays in city councils. By introducing automated defect recognition, mandatory photographic proof for completed work, and direct communication between engineers and citizens, the platform restores transparency and accountability to municipal governance.

Future Scope: In the next phase, the platform can be extended with native Android and iOS mobile applications, automated drone-based aerial road scanning, and an AI-driven WhatsApp chatbot for senior citizens.
